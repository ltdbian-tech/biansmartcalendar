Smart Calendar Privacy Policy

Smart Calendar operates locally on your device. It reads calendar events, contacts (for birthdays), and media images (for the Memories feature) only to display them inside the app.

Data We Access

  • Calendar: Read & write events you explicitly create or view.
  • Contacts (Birthdays): Read-only access to contacts' birthday fields.
  • Photos: Read-only access to images taken on the selected date to surface Memories.
  • Audio (Microphone): Used solely for on-device voice commands.
  • Notifications: Used for optional reminders (if you enable them).

Data Storage & Transfer

No personal data is sent to external servers by default. All processing (including ML suggestions) is performed on-device.

Crash Reporting

If enabled, anonymized crash logs may be sent to Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability. These logs exclude calendar event titles, contact names, or photo contents.

Your Choices

  • You can revoke permissions anytime in system settings.
  • Declining a permission only disables the related feature; the rest of the app works.

Third-Party Libraries

We use: AndroidX libraries, Glide (image loading), Room (local database), TensorFlow Lite (on-device ML), Firebase Crashlytics (optional crash reports).
